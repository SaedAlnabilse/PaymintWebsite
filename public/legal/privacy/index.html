Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mintcom LLC ("Mintcom", "we", "us", or "our"), operator of the Mintcom POS application, collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional Point of Sale experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business details provided during registration.
  • Transaction Data: Sales records, order history, and payment method types (e.g., Cash/Card) processed through the app.
  • Employee Data: Name and PIN codes created for staff management.
  • Images: Photos you choose to capture or select from your device to use as product, menu-item, or business logo images.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Device model, OS version, app version, IP address, unique device identifiers, logs, and crash diagnostics used for security, stability, and app functionality.

2. Hardware Permissions & Usage

Our app requires specific hardware permissions to function as a POS system:

  • Bluetooth: Used solely to discover, pair, and connect to external thermal receipt printers. We do not use Bluetooth for location tracking.
  • Camera: Used to scan QR codes for server configuration, scan barcodes for adding products to the cart, and capture images for menu items. No images are stored or transmitted without your action.
  • Photos & Media: Used only when you choose to select an existing image from your device's photo library for a product, menu item, or business logo.
  • Local Network: Used to discover and connect to receipt and kitchen printers on your local network.
  • Local Storage: Used to store app settings, cached data, and temporary logs to ensure performance during network instability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Manage your account and provide customer support.
  • Process and record sales transactions accurately.
  • Generate business reports and analytics for store owners.
  • Improve app performance and security.
  • Send critical system notifications (e.g., low stock alerts).

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal or business data to third parties. Data is shared only with:

  • Your specific backend server instance (e.g., your-store.mintcompos.com).
  • Payment processors if you choose to integrate electronic payments.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics, which processes crash logs, diagnostics, and a device identifier on our behalf to help us monitor and improve app stability. This data is not used to track you across apps or services.

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect your data during transit.

5. Account & Data Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing our support team at support@mintcompos.com from the email address registered to your account, or by using the in-app account deletion option where available. Please include your business name and the email or phone number registered to your account so we can verify the request.

Once your identity is verified, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days. Some records may be retained for a longer period only where required for legal, tax, accounting, billing, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes, after which they are securely deleted.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via the app or email.

7. Children's Privacy

Mintcom is intended for business use and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
